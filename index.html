What is this module about?

This sensibilization module invites you to explore familiar teaching situations in which digital data and algorithmic systems influence assessment, differentiation and the interpretation of student engagement.

It is designed to raise awareness and stimulate critical reflection—not to test your knowledge or prescribe a single correct answer.

Your role: Imagine you are discussing these situations with student teachers in a teacher education seminar.

About this resource
Data & Algorithms in Teaching is an adaptable sensibilization resource for teacher educators. The scenarios are illustrative and intended to prompt discussion; they do not describe a specific product or school.
